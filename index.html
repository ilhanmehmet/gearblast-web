📖 Gear Blast Nasıl Oynanır?

Gear Blast, dişli temalı bir bulmaca ve bölge fethetme oyunudur. Klasik ve Macera modlarında renkli blokları ızgaraya yerleştirip satır ve sütunları temizlersiniz; Dişli Bölgesi modunda ise dünya haritasında ülke ülke, şehir şehir toprak fethedersiniz. Her mod için ayrı bir skor tablosu vardır. Kurallar basit; ustalaşmak ise strateji ister.

🎮 Temel Oynanış (Klasik ve Macera)

Her turda ekranın altında 3 parça görürsünüz. Bir parçayı parmağınızla sürükleyip ızgaraya bırakırsınız. Parça yerleştiğinde:

  • 🔹 Tam dolan satır veya sütunlar patlar ve puan kazanırsınız.
  • 🔹 Aynı hamlede birden fazla satır/sütun temizlerseniz combo oluşur; combo ne kadar yüksekse o hamle o kadar değerli olur.
  • 🔹 Üç parçayı da yerleştirdiğinizde yeni bir üçlü gelir.

Oyun, ızgaraya hiçbir parça sığmadığında biter. Bu yüzden her hamlede hem puan hem de boş alan düşünmeniz gerekir.

🎯 Neden Gear Blast?

  • 🎲 Sıfır şans, saf strateji: Her hamle sizin kararınız.
  • 🧠 Akıllı parça sistemi: Bloklar tahtanıza uyum sağlar; oyun, siz stratejik bir hata yapana kadar bitmez.
  • 📼 VAR (Hata Analizi): Sıkıştığınızda oyun hemen bitmez; son hamleleriniz geri sarılır, hatanız gösterilir ve kaldığınız yerden devam edebilirsiniz.
  • ↩️ Geri Al: Klasik modda son hamlenizi geri alırsınız.
  • 📴 İnternetsiz oynanır: Klasik, Macera ve Dişli Bölgesi internet olmadan çalışır; yalnızca skor tabloları için çevrimiçi olmanız gerekir.
  • 💰 Cüzdan ve anahtar sekmesi: altın, mücevher ve anahtarlarınız her an elinizin altında.
  • 🏅 Lig sistemi: 5 lig, ligden düşme yok.
  • 👤 60'tan fazla avatar ve profil oluşturmadan da oynayabilme.
  • 🌐 40'tan fazla dil ve 3 hamlelik hızlı eğitim.

Klasik ve Macera'da oynadıkça anahtar, altın ve mücevher kazanırsınız; hepsi cüzdanınıza gider. Anahtarlarla Dişli Bölgesi haritasındaki yeni şehirlerin kilidini açarsınız. Ne kadar çok oynarsanız dünya o kadar açılır.

1. Klasik

Süre sınırı olmayan saf skor oyunu. Kombo zinciri kurar, kombo bataryasını doldurur ve girdap patlamalarını tetiklersiniz. Yüksek kombo zincirlerinde karakter, araç ve sandık kazanırsınız. Undo (geri al) hakları bu modda aktiftir. En yüksek skorunuz skor tablosuna yazılır ve lig rozetinizi belirler. Kazandığınız her puan sizi bir sonraki harita anahtarına yaklaştırır.

2. Macera

3000 bölümlük bir yolculuk. Her bölümün kendine özel görünümü ve hedefi vardır. Haritadan sıradaki bölüme dokunarak oyuna girersiniz. Amacınız üstteki renk barlarını doldurmak veya engel taşlarını temizlemektir. Süre ve hamle sınırı yoktur. Bölüm bitince zafer animasyonu ve robot koleksiyonu sizi bekler. Skor tablosunda en yüksek bölümünüz yarışır. Bölüm geçtikçe harita anahtarı kazanırsınız.

3. Dişli Bölgesi

Dünyayı şehir şehir fethedin. Dünya haritasından bir ülke, sonra bir şehir seçip rakiplerle dolu arenaya girersiniz. Robotunuzla kendi alanınızdan çıkıp geri dönerek yeni toprak kazanırsınız; haritanın tamamını boyayınca şehri bitirmiş olursunuz. Skorunuz bitirdiğiniz ülke ve şehir sayısıdır. Klasik ve Macera'da kazandığınız anahtarlarla yeni şehirlerin kilidini açarsınız. Ayrıntılar aşağıdaki Dişli Bölgesi bölümünde.

  • ❤️ Canlandırma: Hamle kalmadığında reklam izleyerek canlanıp oyuna devam edebilirsiniz. Bir oyunda en fazla 4 kez canlanabilirsiniz. Reklamsız pakette canlanma reklam izlemeden anında gerçekleşir. 4 hakkınız bittiğinde ya da "Geç" dediğinizde oyun biter ve skorunuz kaydedilir.

🧭 Arena

  • 🔹 Karakterinizi parmağınızla yönlendirirsiniz.

📖 How to Play Gear Blast?

Gear Blast is a gear-themed puzzle and territory-capture game. In Classic and Adventure you place colorful blocks on a grid and clear rows and columns; in Gear Zone you capture land country by country and city by city on a world map. Each mode has its own leaderboard. The rules are simple, but mastering them takes strategy.

🎮 Basic Gameplay (Classic & Adventure)

Each round you see 3 pieces at the bottom of the screen. Drag a piece onto the grid and drop it. When a piece lands:

  • 🔹 Full rows or columns blast and you earn points.
  • 🔹 Clear several rows/columns in one move to create a combo; the higher the combo, the more valuable the move.
  • 🔹 When all three pieces are placed, a new set arrives.

The game ends when no piece fits on the grid, so every move needs both points and free space in mind.

🎯 Why Gear Blast?

  • 🎲 Zero luck, pure strategy: Every move is your decision.
  • 🧠 Smart piece system: Blocks adapt to your board; the game only ends when you make a strategic mistake.
  • 📼 VAR (Mistake Analysis): When you get stuck the game doesn't end right away; your last moves are rewound, your mistake is shown and you continue where you left off.
  • ↩️ Undo: Take back your last move in Classic mode.
  • 📴 Play offline: Classic, Adventure and Gear Zone work without internet; you only need to be online for the leaderboards.
  • 💰 Wallet and key tab: your gold, gems and keys are always at hand.
  • 🏅 League system: 5 leagues, no demotion.
  • 👤 60+ avatars and you can play without creating a profile.
  • 🌐 40+ languages and a quick 3-move tutorial.

🚀 First Launch

When you open the app for the first time after downloading, you will see, in order:

  • 🔔 Notification permission (Android 13+) and a privacy/data-usage approval.
  • 🎓 A short tutorial: follow the hand icon to make 3 moves.
  • 🗺️ After the tutorial the game starts directly in Adventure mode. You can go back to the main menu and pick any mode.
  • 🌐 Your game language is chosen from your phone's language (you can change it later).
  • ✍️ You choose your username (3–22 characters; shown on leaderboards) and avatar when you enter online features such as the leaderboard or profile.

On later launches you go straight to the main menu; no need to register again.

🏠 Main Menu

The main menu is the hub for every mode and feature:

  • 🎮 Game modes: Classic, Adventure and Gear Zone.
  • 👤 Your profile card: avatar and username.
  • 💰 Wallet and key tab: your gold, gems and keys.
  • 🏆 Leaderboard: separate rankings for three modes and the Global List.
  • 🛒 Icons along the bottom: shop, profile, settings and archive (badges and stats).

PLAY, EARN KEYS, UNLOCK THE WORLD

In Classic and Adventure you earn keys, gold and gems as you play, and they all go to your wallet. Keys unlock new cities on the Gear Zone map. The more you play, the more of the world opens up.

1. Classic

A pure score game with no time limit. Chain combos, charge the combo battery and trigger vortex blasts. High combo chains win you robots and chests. Undo credits are active in this mode. Your best score goes to the leaderboard and sets your league badge. Every point you score brings you closer to your next map key.

2. Adventure

A journey through 3000 levels. Every level has its own look and goal. Tap the next level on the map to enter. Fill the color bars at the top or clear the obstacle blocks. There are no time or move limits. A victory animation and your robot collection await after each level. Your highest level is ranked on the leaderboard. Passing levels also earns you map keys.

3. Gear Zone

Conquer the world city by city. Pick a country on the world map, then a city, and enter an arena full of rivals. Leave your own territory and loop back to claim new land; paint the whole map to finish the city. Your score is the number of countries and cities you complete. Use the keys you earn in Classic and Adventure to unlock new cities. Details in the Gear Zone section below.

⏸️ Pause Menu

When you pause during a game you see these options:

  • ▶️ Resume: Go back to the game.
  • 🔄 New Game: Start over in the same mode.
  • ⚙️ Settings: Sound, music and vibration.
  • 🏠 Main Menu: Exit (confirmation asked, the saved game may be lost).
  • 🗺️ Map: Back to the Adventure map (Adventure only).

🔄 Deadlock, Error Analysis & Revive (Classic & Adventure)

The game ends when no move is left on the grid. Then:

  • 🔍 Error analysis: your last moves are reviewed, showing wrong placements and better alternatives.
  • ❤️ Revive: when no moves are left you can watch an ad to revive and continue. You can revive up to 4 times per game. With the ad-free pack revives happen instantly without an ad. When your 4 revives are used, or you choose "Skip", the game ends and your score is saved.

🌍 Gear Zone Guide

Gear Zone is a territory-capture mode played on the world map. You pick a country, then open one of its cities and enter the arena.

🧭 Arena

  • 🔹 You steer your character with your finger.
  • 🔹 Leave your territory, draw a loop and return: the land inside becomes yours.
  • 🔹 If the trail you draw outside is cut, you are out; you can cut rivals' trails to eliminate them too.
  • 🔹 Paint the whole map and the city is complete, earning rewards.
  • ❤️ Revive: up to 3 times per round, by watching an ad. The offer appears if you own at least 60% of the map when you are eliminated.

🔑 Map Keys

Countries are open from the start; cities are locked. The smallest city of your own country starts open. You can open a city in three ways:

MethodCost
Keys1–5 keys, by the city's size
Gems20 gems
Gold10,000 gold

🗝️ How to Earn Keys

  • 🔹 Classic: in one game the first key comes at 25,000 points, and each next one needs 10,000 more.
  • 🔹 Adventure: each odd-numbered level (1, 3, 5, 7...) passed for the first time gives a key.
  • 🔹 Daily quests and the free chest can also give keys.

🪙 Gold, Gems & Rewards

  • 🔹 You collect gold and gems in the arena; rewards are also added to your wallet after a round and when you finish a city.
  • 🔹 The daily login bonus is a 7-day streak.
  • 🔹 4 daily quests refresh every day; you claim the reward yourself when done.
  • 🔹 Gold packs are bought in the shop with gems (50 gems = 50,000 gold). Gem packs are sold for real money.

🤖 Characters, Vehicles & Skills

  • 🤖 70 characters: robots and animals. Some unlock as you progress, others are bought with gold or gems. Your selected character is shown next to your name on the Gear Zone leaderboard.
  • 🛴 40 vehicles (10 kinds, 4 versions): each gives a speed, magnet or gold bonus.
  • ⚡ 6 skills: Shield, Sprint, Dash, Shock Wave, Paint Bomb and Magnet. Upgrade them with gold; each level adds +5% power, up to +50%.
  • 🎁 Chests: the free chest opens every 4 hours (an ad shortens the wait by 1 hour). Gold and gem chests are also available; the content odds are written on the chest card.

📊 Score & Saving

Your score is the number of countries and cities you complete (for example "Country 2 / City 37"). Your Gear Zone progress (gold, gems, characters, vehicles) is stored on your device; your score is sent to the leaderboard.

Game Modes & Gameplay

There are three modes: Classic, Adventure and Gear Zone. Classic is best to learn combos and scoring, Adventure for a relaxed pace, and Gear Zone for capturing territory on the map.

Adventure moves through a 3000-level map with no time or move limit, focused on filling the color bars and collecting robots. Classic is a pure score game with the combo battery, vortex blasts and league badges.

When you clear vertical and horizontal lines in the same move, a cross blast happens and gives you a very high combo score bonus.

When you run out of moves, the error analysis screen reviews your last moves and shows where you went wrong and the better alternatives. Then you can watch an ad (no ad with the ad-free pack) to revive and continue. In Classic and Adventure you can revive up to 4 times per game; when they run out, or you choose "Skip", the game ends.

Gear Zone

Cities are opened with keys, gems or gold. You can earn keys by playing Classic and Adventure; daily quests and the free chest can also give keys.

The Gear Zone leaderboard draws your currently selected character next to your name. When you change characters your score is sent again and the list updates.

Your gold, gems, characters and vehicles are stored on your device. Your score stays on the leaderboard.

Undo & Shop

In Classic you can undo your last move. You start the game with 3 rights; you can buy more as packs in the shop and load them into the active slots from your inventory. There is no undo in Adventure.

When you buy the ad-free pack in the shop, the banner ads at the bottom of the game and the full-screen ads on revives and transitions are removed completely; revives happen instantly without watching an ad.
